Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what the Fader app (Fader: Learn by Reading, Android, package com.overd0se.fader) does with your data. Fader is developed by Catalyst Sol (LUSHIN DAVID VADIMOVICH), Republic of Korea. The short version: there are no accounts, your reading stays on your phone, and the only things that leave it are anonymous statistics (which you can switch off) and your subscription purchase.

No accounts

Fader has no sign-up or login. The app never asks for your name, email address or phone number.

What stays on your phone

Your word progress, reading history, settings and reminder schedule are stored on your device only. They are never sent anywhere. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Purchases

Fader Pro subscriptions are bought through Google Play Billing. Your payment details stay with Google and we never see them.

To make the subscription work and to let you restore it, we use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA), which processes the purchase on our behalf. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and a random anonymous customer ID created by the app.

Anonymous statistics

Fader sends anonymous usage statistics to PostHog, using PostHog Cloud EU servers in the European Union. This is on by default. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → About → «Anonymous statistics». When you turn it off, the app stops collecting statistics at once.

The statistics are these events:

  • onboarding started;
  • onboarding completed (the level you chose);
  • a text or chat opened and finished (its type, level and topic);
  • a tap on a word (only that a tap happened and the text’s type and level, not which word);
  • the reading slider released (its position, 0 to 100);
  • the daily free limit reached;
  • the subscription screen opened (and from where);
  • a trial started or a subscription bought (which plan);
  • the first finished text of a day (your streak length).

Every event also carries your language pair and whether you have Fader Pro.

The analytics library adds some technical details: your device model, Android version, app version, phone language and time zone, screen size, and a random installation ID.

We do not collect your IP address (it is discarded on arrival), your location, your advertising ID, your contacts, the words or texts themselves, or anything you type. There is no session recording, no tap tracking and no tracking across apps.

PostHog keeps the statistics on our behalf only as long as needed to understand how the app is used. Because the statistics are anonymous, we usually cannot link them to a person, so the effective control is the switch in Settings. If you have a question or a request about your data, write to us (see Contact below).

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. There is no push server.

Listening

Listening uses your phone’s built-in text-to-speech. The app sends nothing to a server for it.

What we do not do

  • No ads.
  • We never sell your data.
  • No tracking across apps.

Children

Fader is for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.
